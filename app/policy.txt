STORE POLICY
Last updated: 1 November 2026
 
All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include GST unless stated otherwise.
 
------------------------------------------------------------
1. ORDER PROCESSING AND SHIPPING
------------------------------------------------------------
- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of payment confirmation.
  Orders placed after 6:00 PM IST, or on Sundays and public holidays, are
  processed on the next business day.
- Estimated delivery times after dispatch:
    Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad):
      2-4 business days
    Rest of India (tier 2 and tier 3 cities):
      4-7 business days
    Remote areas (North-East, J&K, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep):
      7-10 business days
- Shipping charges:
    Orders of Rs. 999 or more: free shipping
    Orders below Rs. 999: flat Rs. 79 shipping fee
- A tracking number is shared once the order status changes to "shipped".
- Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Delays caused by weather, strikes,
  or courier disruptions are outside our control.
 
------------------------------------------------------------
2. ORDER CANCELLATION
------------------------------------------------------------
- An order can be cancelled for a full refund while its status is
  "processing".
- Once an order is "shipped", it can no longer be cancelled. The customer
  may refuse delivery or request a return after delivery under the return
  policy below.
- Refunds for cancelled prepaid orders go back to the original payment
  method within 5-7 business days.
 
------------------------------------------------------------
3. RETURN POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------
- Return window: 30 days from the delivery date. Requests made after 30 days
  are not eligible for a return or refund.
- Items must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging with all tags
  and accessories included.
- Only orders with status "delivered" can be returned.
- Items marked non-returnable on the product page (e.g. innerwear, opened
  personal-care items, gift cards, and final-sale items) cannot be returned
  unless they arrive defective or damaged.
 
Footwear:
- Shoes must be unworn to be eligible for return. Shoes may be tried on
  indoors on a clean surface only.
- Shoes showing signs of wear (scuffed soles, dirt, creasing from use, odour,
  or missing original box) will be rejected, and the customer may be asked to
  collect them at their own cost.
 
Defective, damaged, or wrong items:
- Report within 7 days of delivery with clear photos of the item and
  packaging. We will arrange a free pickup and offer a replacement or a full
  refund.
 
------------------------------------------------------------
4. REFUNDS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Refund amount = price paid for the returned item(s). Original shipping fees
  are refunded only when the return is due to our error (wrong, defective, or
  damaged item).
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days
  after the returned item passes inspection.
- Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer; the customer must
  provide valid bank details to our team through the secure form, never over
  chat.
 
Refund approval limits:
- Refunds of Rs. 2,000 or less may be approved by the support agent when the
  return meets the policy above.
- Refunds ABOVE Rs. 2,000 (i.e. more than Rs. 2,000, which is 200,000 paise)
  REQUIRE HUMAN ESCALATION. The support agent must not approve or promise
  these refunds. Instead, create an escalation, tell the customer that a
  member of our team will review the request, and give a response time of
  1-2 business days.
- Escalation also applies when a case falls outside this policy, such as
  returns after 30 days, suspected fraud, repeated refund requests, or
  customer disputes the agent cannot resolve.
 
------------------------------------------------------------
5. EXCHANGES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Exchanges for a different size or colour are available within the same
  30-day window, subject to stock. If the replacement is unavailable, a
  refund is issued under Section 4.
